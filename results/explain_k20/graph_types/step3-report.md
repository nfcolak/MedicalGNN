# Step 3: clinical context and confidence axis

## Method (rules fixed in the script before any difference was looked at)

Test fold, K=20, primary_dx_v4, fair_v10t, 1,345 visits of 1,000 patients, 3 seeds per model. Descriptive and exploratory: nothing is selected or tuned from these numbers. d = CEI - comparator per visit (seed-averaged, visit counted only if the source is ok in >= 2 of 3 seeds); positive d = CEI scores higher. Headline score `del` = deletion_mean_change_vs_random (higher is better). For `fid-` (fidelity-) lower is better, so there a negative d favours CEI. CEI = cei_final (label smoothing 0.1) and, separately, cei_R1_ls0 (no label smoothing). Comparators: GSAT and GraphCare. Columns IG / Grad / GNNExp compare the same explainer in both models; `built-in` is CEI built-in vs GSAT built-in; `built-in vs GSAT IG` and `built-in vs GC Grad` compare the CEI built-in explanation with the comparator's best post-hoc explainer (GraphCare's built-in is not usable, so it is not compared). 95% CIs: patient-cluster bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seed 20261025. **Bold** = CI excludes 0 (n >= 30). † = 10 <= n < 30, shown but not interpreted; cells under 10 visits are not printed. `dConf` = mean confidence of CEI minus mean confidence of the comparator in the stratum (the confounder).

Class groups (fixed up front): cardiac = Heart failure, Non-ST elevation (NSTEMI) myocardial infarction, Unspecified atrial fibrillation; infection = Sepsis, UTI or pyelonephritis, Skin or soft-tissue infection, Acute pharyngitis, Lower respiratory disease; injury_pain = Head injury, Limb injury or pain, Back or spine pain; gastrointestinal = GI bleed, Unsp intestnl obst; metabolic_renal_heme = Acute kidney failure, Anemia, Diabetes mellitus, Hypokalemia; neuro_psych_substance = Epilepsy, Major depressive disorder, Alcohol abuse with intoxication.

Correct = at least 2 of the 3 seeds predict the true class. Seed-stable = all 3 seeds predict the same class. Confidence = seed-mean predicted-class probability.

Confidence-adjusted ('adj') = regression adjustment: weighted least squares of d on a quadratic in (CEI confidence, comparator confidence), refitted in every bootstrap draw; the adjusted stratum mean is what remains after removing what the two confidences alone predict (same overall mean as raw). The 3 x 3 matched grid (tertile of CEI confidence x tertile of comparator confidence) is the non-parametric confidence-matched comparison. Edges of the fixed confidence bins:
